### IT Practical Training – North Electricity Distribution Company (NEDCO)

Successfully completed a six-month practical training program within the **Information Technology (IT) Department at North Electricity Distribution Company (NEDCO)**.

Throughout this experience, I gained valuable exposure to real-world enterprise IT environments and developed a deeper understanding of how technology supports large-scale organizational operations.

The training provided practical insights into several key areas, including:

- **Database Management:** Understanding database structures, data handling, validation, and information management.
- **APIs & Enterprise Systems:** Exploring how software systems communicate, exchange data, and integrate with organizational workflows.
- **Computer Networking:** Learning networking fundamentals, IP addressing, network infrastructure, and connectivity.
- **Cybersecurity:** Gaining knowledge of system security, access control, encryption, firewalls, and secure remote access.
- **IT Infrastructure:** Working with operating systems, hardware components, servers, and enterprise technology.
- **Technical Support & Troubleshooting:** Developing practical problem-solving skills through hardware and software troubleshooting, system configuration, and user support.

Beyond the technical knowledge, this experience strengthened my analytical thinking, troubleshooting abilities, teamwork, and understanding of professional IT environments.

**This training was an important step in bridging academic knowledge with practical industry experience, strengthening my technical foundation, and preparing me for future challenges in the field of Information Technology.**
